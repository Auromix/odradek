Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: R2 end-effector concept board for the Odradek open-source desktop arm.
Primary request: Redesign a modular FOUR-DOF end effector with exactly FOUR luminous grasping petals, ONE circular LED pixel screen, and TWO fisheye cameras vertically above and below the screen. Show full opening, real grasp, fully closed empty storage, and wrist roll.
Input roles: Image 1 original game front view supplies thin articulated light panels and mechanical creature character, NOT its five-panel count; Image 2 original pointing pose supplies finger-like folding and slender linkages; Image 3 XPENG logo supplies paired wing composition ONLY, NOT its wordmark or exact logo shape; Image 4 supplies fine pen and marker drawing style.
Fixed morphology, crucial: FOUR petal fingers arranged as TWO CLOSE NEIGHBOR PAIRS, one pair on the left and one pair on the right of the central screen. In front view the upper-left and lower-left petals are close to one another in angle, and mirrored upper-right/lower-right petals form the other close pair. Broad blank sectors above and below leave room for the two cameras. Only LEFT-RIGHT mirror symmetry: the upper two petals are longer, swept outward at a shallow upward angle; the lower two are shorter, swept outward at a steeper downward angle. Upper versus lower geometry visibly differs, so the head is NOT 180-degree rotationally symmetric, NOT an equal-arm plus sign, NOT four equidistant radial blades. Angular softly tapered wing-shaped light surfaces, narrow segmented structural stems, raised protective rims; upper petals about 20 percent longer than lower. Compact central dark palm. Graphite frames, restrained warm-white light faces, small bone-white protective covers, muted amber accents.
Each light petal IS the gripper finger: inner dark elastomer contact pads, outer diffuse LED surface. Four independent root bending coordinates q1 q2 q3 q4, ONE per finger. A two-segment finger is allowed only through mechanically coupled linkage, no independently driven extra joints. No second conventional gripper.
Central face: one CIRCULAR BLACK LED DOT-MATRIX SCREEN, thin NON-LUMINOUS dark bezel. Screen graphics include a ring drawn with amber pixels just inside the screen edge, or a dot/arrow. Never a physical illuminated ring, no separate annular light, no central spotlight or big central lens. TWO small convex dark fisheye lenses at exactly local twelve and six o'clock above/below the screen, both clearly visible in open view. All task lights are on the four petals.
Layout: clean landscape 1536x1024 pen-and-marker industrial design review board with four zones:
1 top-left largest: direct FRONT VIEW fully open head showing the paired left/right wings and two cameras, with a fine vertical symmetry construction line. Label "OPEN / 4 INDEPENDENT FINGERS".
2 top-right: same head three-quarter view grasping a plain modest rectangular test block with the inner pads of the same four petals. Light panels remain visible outward. Label "GRIP / HOLD".
3 bottom-left: same head EMPTY, FULLY CLOSED, four light petals have wrapped forward together as a compact protective pod, tips meet with narrow real seams, no central hole, no object, no extra lid. Screen and cameras deliberately hidden by closed petals. Back wrist housing may be visible. Label "FULLY CLOSED / EMPTY" and small "SCREEN + CAMERAS OCCLUDED".
4 bottom-right: three small FRONT silhouettes of the OPEN same head at neutral, clockwise tilted and counterclockwise tilted angles. Short curved double-ended arrows indicate rotation in its face plane; BOTH cameras and ALL four fingers rotate together. A central axis-dot symbol identifies arm J7 pointing normal out of the face. Label "J7 / WHOLE-HEAD ROLL" and "ARM AXIS, NOT A FIFTH HEAD DOF". Do not imply endless rotation.
Typography: title "ODRADEK / R2 PETAL HEAD"; bottom "4 FINGER DOF / 1 PER FINGER / CONCEPT GEOMETRY".
Style: crisp black fine pen strokes, light blue-gray marker shading, clean warm-white paper, moderate construction lines, generous whitespace. Detailed but readable; no dimensions or numerical load claims. No XPENG branding, people, cartoon eyes, separate physical halo, floating hardware, or invented fifth petal. Invariants across all views are petal identity, head shape, camera placement, no physical light ring. This is exploratory concept art, not CAD.
