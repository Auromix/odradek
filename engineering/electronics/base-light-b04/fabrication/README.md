# Fabrication review candidate

Real KiCad Gerber and Excellon exports. Nominal FR4 50 x 12 x 1.6 mm, 2 copper layers; two NPTH diameter 2.2 mm. LED lands follow the cited manufacturer's recommendation. Back harness lands have no paste opening and require separate controlled wire soldering/strain relief. No on-board current limiter: connect only to service B04 J3.

Do not treat this export as production release: PCB stack tolerance, stencil, surface finish, solder process, electrical sample and enclosure/optical tests remain. See ../README.md and ../verification.json.
